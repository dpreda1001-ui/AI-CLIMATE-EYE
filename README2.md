# AI CLIMATE EYE

A decentralized, self-improving AI platform for impartial, open-source climate trajectory assessment.

[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-Apache%202.0-blue.svg)](LICENSE)

---

## Overview

Global climate governance is fragmented, politically influenced, and often driven by short-term economic interests. The AI CLIMATE EYE is a non-commercial, open-source digital public infrastructure designed to bypass these biases.

It continuously ingests open Earth observation data (Copernicus, NASA, UNFCCC) and generates Autonomous Eco-Climate Trajectories (AECT) — cryptographically signed, publicly broadcast projections of the long-term ecological and climatic consequences of human actions.

The system is:

- Self-improving – autonomously writes and optimizes its own code within strict physical constraints.
- Decentralized – runs across a global network of distributed hardware to prevent single points of failure or bias.
- Transparent – all outputs are published via an unmetered public API and a Transparent Data Channel (TDC) in open formats (GeoJSON, NetCDF, JSON-LD).

---

## Key Features

- Ensemble MoE Engine – Multiple independent models reach consensus, filtering out political and corporate noise.
- Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) – Core logic is hard-anchored to thermodynamic laws and mass conservation.
- Self-Improvement Loop – Autonomous code generation with built-in safety circuit breakers and public audit trails.
- Open Data Only – Ingests only public, non-proprietary datasets (Copernicus, NASA, UNFCCC).
- Glass-Box Outputs – All AECT metrics are cryptographically signed and broadcast openly.
